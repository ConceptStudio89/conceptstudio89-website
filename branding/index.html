A LITTLE MORE DETAIL

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is included in a brand identity project?

Depending on the scope, we develop the brand concept, logo system, typography, color palette, graphic language, key applications and a practical identity guide.

Can you refresh an existing identity?

Yes. We can evaluate your current visual language and develop a focused refresh or a full identity redesign.

Do you create packaging and launch graphics too?

Yes. Packaging concepts, social graphics and presentation materials can be included as applications or commissioned as additional graphic design work.
