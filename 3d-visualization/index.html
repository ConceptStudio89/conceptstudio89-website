A LITTLE MORE DETAIL

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do you manufacture or install exhibition booths?

No. ConceptStudio 89 provides design and visualization services. Production, materials and on site installation are managed separately by a manufacturer or builder.

Can you render an existing design?

Yes. With the appropriate files and references, we can help visualize an existing design and improve how it is presented.

Can you connect branding with 3D environments?

Yes. We combine graphic design and spatial visualization so the identity feels consistent across digital material, products and physical spaces.
